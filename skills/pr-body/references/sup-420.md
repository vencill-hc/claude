A real body, drafted by Claude and edited by her (2026-08-11). The diff between the two is the source of most rulings in SKILL.md. Both versions verbatim; her text is never corrected.

## The draft

> Bug fixes to the job function keyword table, staged ahead of the taxonomy swap so the swap's delta shrinks to the changes that are actually about taxonomy. Two root causes cover nearly everything here. The match template appends `\w*` to every key, so short keys read unrelated words (`pr` reads principal, propietario, profesor; `coo` reads coordinator and cook). And first-match-wins makes table position priority, so a general key above a specific one steals its titles (`controller` above `quality control`). Nothing in this PR touches the enum, the schemas, or any consumer contract: the only production effect is which label a title gets.
>
> Every fix lands on the replacement taxonomy's ruling for the same key, checked against its spec, so a row that moves now is already where the swap will put it. Rows move once.
>
> | Section | Change | Rows moved |
> |---|---|---|
> | Silent nulls | `_am_` sentinel (the normalizer rewrites "account manager" to a keyless token, so every Account Manager parsed to null), `fulfil` stem for both spellings, `recruitment`, `marine corps` | 8,607,369 |
> | `pr` removal | the single worst key: everything `pr`-prefixed with no better match landed in Media & Communications | 45,948,659 |
> | Prefix explosions | `ai`, `art`, `media`, `information`, `test` removed (existing narrower keys keep the real vocabulary); `pm`/`gm`/`coo` become exact-token sentinels; `lawn` exclusion blocker; `ipo` carve-out to Finance; three normalization-dead keys deleted | 36,412,617 |
> | Ordering fixes | accounts payable/receivable/assistant carve-outs above `account`; `operating room` above `operating`; `quality control` above `controller`; `police inspector` above `inspector`; data science/scientist/architect repoint to Data / Analytics | 3,395,891 |
> | Off-axis tokens | `intern`, `student`, `associate`, bare `assistant`, `general business` removed: engagement and level words, not functions. `intern` was also eating internal/international titles | 51,699,266 |
>
> Total: 146,063,802 row-moves, 10.26% of the 1.42B-row corpus (a title moving at two boundaries counts twice; only a sliver do, so the distinct-row total is marginally lower). The character of the delta: 7.5M titles gain a label, 116.2M drop a wrong label to null, 22.3M move between labels.
>
> The de-labeling share deserves the plain reading: 8% of the corpus is wearing labels these bugs invented. Principals and propietarios as Media & Communications, coordinators and cooks as Operations, interns as Education. Null is the correct verdict in the current vocabulary, and it is also where the replacement taxonomy expects these rows, so the swap re-labels a large share of them properly instead of fighting stale wrong answers.
>
> Label movements worth calling out by name:
>
> - General Manager reads Management now, not Business Development: 5.2M rows, the largest single flip.
> - Recruitment Consultant reads Human Resources, not Consulting, matching the precedence the table already claimed for recruiting terms.
> - Accounts payable/receivable move from Sales to Accounting (1.8M), and data scientists from Engineering to Data / Analytics (1.4M).
>
> Numbers come from a full-corpus replay of every commit boundary: each commit's table is extracted from git and replayed over all 1.42B rows, with the fast matcher self-checked against the real parser on the first 2,000 eligible rows. The per-transition breakdown (which label went where, with example titles) lives in the workpod thread (`vvencill-job-function-taxonomy/pr-stack-deltas.txt`); ask and I'll pull any slice into the review.
>
> Each commit is one bug class, written test-first; where a fix intentionally changes a pinned label, the expectation change is its own commit immediately after the source commit. Full suite green at every commit.
>
> Relabel scope: proposed at merge time per the team's decide-per-release rule. The changed keys are disjoint, so one relabel pass covers the whole PR.

## The rulings, from the diff

1. Cut whole: the scope-reassurance sentence, the "rows move once" paragraph, the methodology paragraph, the commit-hygiene paragraph, the relabel-scope paragraph. All assurance, none of it scopes the review. Her words: "often methodology is not relevant to the actual code being reviewed and thus I cut for brevity"; it merits inclusion only when "focal to the code changes being reviewed".
2. Cut whole: the interpretation paragraph ("the de-labeling share deserves the plain reading"). Her words: "redundancy makes this harder to read, especially when the way in which the numbers are presented are vaugely or subtely different. I prefer the objective statement and not the narritive-driven explanation. Narrative has a place in the opening paragraph of the PR body but essentially nowhere else."
3. Restructured: root causes to a numbered list, their examples to stacked bullets, the delta decomposition to bullets. Prose stops the moment enumeration starts.
4. Compressed: the double-counting parenthetical to "~". Her words: "if it's not important enough to include as a main text sentence, cut it"; examples that break the flow go to "bullets or charts or supporting figures, not in parentheticals". Uncertainty about code performance is called out directly or fixed before review.
5. Word-level: "reads Management" to "is labeled Management" (her ruling: "any time 'reads' is used to mean 'is labeled', that is incorrect. Coinage is rarely useful, esecially when the coined word is generic"); "the taxonomy swap" to "the larger taxonomy swap" (one word of orientation); "move from" to "moved from".
6. Footer: both tickets slash-separated; her 🦋 revised-by line appended after the 🤖 line.
